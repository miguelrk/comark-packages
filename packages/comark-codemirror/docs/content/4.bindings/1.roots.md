Comark renderers resolve bindings against `{ frontmatter, meta, data, props }`. The editor completes the same roots, segment by segment:

| Root | Keys come from | Offered |
|---|---|---|
| `frontmatter` | this document's frontmatter, parsed live | always |
| `props` | the nearest enclosing component that has its own attributes: its attribute block, its YAML props block, manifest defaults | inside such a component (not on its own opener) |
| `data` | the host's `data` / `dataSchema` | when configured |
| `meta` | the host's `meta` / `metaSchema`, plus plugins (`title`, `description`, `toc`, `summary`) | when configured or a plugin adds keys |
| loop variables | `scope` of an enclosing component, e.g. `::for` | inside it |

```md
---
site:
  name: My Blog
---

Welcome to {{ frontmatter.site.name || our site }}.

::card{title="Hello" variant="ghost"}
:badge{:label="props.title"}
::
```

- Objects show `›` and drill in with <kbd>→</kbd> or <kbd>.</kbd>; leaves show their current value.
- After a complete path and a space, `||` adds a fallback; the next menu offers the current value.
- `:prop="…"` on a boolean prop also offers `true` and `false`.
- `::prop="…"` (two-way) only offers writable roots — `data` by default.
- Paths that do not start with a root (`$doc.snippet.link`) are passed through by comark and left alone.

## Lint and hover

A path that leaves a known shape is reported with a suggestion — `frontmatter.titel` → "did you mean `title`?" — and a quick fix. The frontmatter is always known completely; `data` is known completely when you give a schema. Hover shows a path's current value.
