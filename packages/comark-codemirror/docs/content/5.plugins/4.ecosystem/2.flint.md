comark-flint-chart charts.


## What it adds

- **Snippets**: *Flint chart*
- **Components**: `::flint`
- **Fence languages**: `flint`

| `::flint` | `spec`, `backend` (vegalite \| echarts \| chartjs \| plotly \| excel), `theme`, `output` (component \| svg \| img), `width`, `height` | – |
